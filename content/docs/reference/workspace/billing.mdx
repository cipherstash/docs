CipherStash bills per **organization**. Each organization has one plan and one usage allowance, and every workspace in the organization shares them. Workspaces, keysets, client apps, and organization members are unlimited on every plan.

## What is metered

Usage is counted in **encryption operations**, and one operation is one value encrypted. A bulk call that encrypts 500 values counts as 500 operations, the same as 500 single calls. Batching makes encryption faster but does not change the count.

**Decryption is always free** and does not count against your allowance.

Allowances reset monthly.

## Plans

See [pricing](https://cipherstash.com/pricing) for the current plans, their prices, and their allowances.

- **Dev** is free, with a fixed monthly allowance. It has no overage: it stops at its allowance, and you upgrade to keep encrypting.
- **Pro** and **Business** include a monthly allowance, and usage beyond it is billed per 1,000 operations.
- **Enterprise** has custom volume and pricing. Contact [sales@cipherstash.com](mailto:sales@cipherstash.com).

New organizations start on Dev.

### Identity providers

Dev and Pro organizations can use the free OIDC provider that the [Supabase connector](/integrations/supabase) sets up. Business and Enterprise allow unlimited OIDC providers.

## Managing billing

Billing is managed at the organization level, from **Billing** in the [Dashboard](https://dashboard.cipherstash.com) navigation. The billing page shows:

- the organization's current plan, status, and renewal date;
- usage for the current period, with a breakdown by workspace;
- the plan picker, to upgrade or downgrade;
- on Pro and Business, a link to the billing portal for invoices, payment methods, and billing details;
- on Pro and Business, the option to cancel.

The workspace breakdown is informational. All workspaces draw on the organization's single allowance.

## Cancellation

Canceling keeps your plan active until the end of the current billing period, and you can reactivate it before then. After the period ends, the organization moves to Dev.

## Enterprise and AWS Marketplace

Organizations on Enterprise, or subscribed through AWS Marketplace, see an Enterprise card on the billing page instead of the plan picker. Changes to an Enterprise plan go through your account manager.
